Scan your space

Connecting to your computer…
  1. Stand where the robot should start and face the direction it should drive forward.
  2. Tap Start scan, allow the camera, and keep the phone at chest height.
  3. Walk slowly and sweep the phone across walls, furniture, people and pets. Everything the camera sees becomes part of the map.
  4. Tap Finish. The map appears on your computer.

Your camera picture stays on this phone. Only 3D points (no images) go to your computer, directly over your Wi-Fi.
